# ShotMatch User Manual

## Startup
- To begin, create an account by selecting the "Create Account" button. 
- You will be redirected back to the Log In page where you should now type the email and password you just registered with.
- From here, you will be directed to a page with 3 choices:
    - Compare
    - Consistency
    - Historical Graph
- The next few sections will detail each of these three

## Compare
- Allows you to choose between Stephen Curry, Lebron James, and Klay Thompson
- You will have to upload one video from the side and one video from the front.
    - ```There are example angles that you should follow as deviating from those can lead to errors in the score.```
- Once the videos are uploaded, it will take a few seconds for the videos to be processed and you will be redirected to a results screen
- This page will show your overall score compared to the player you selected as well as sub-scores
    - These should indicate what you need to work to improve your shot in order to have similar mechanics to the best in the world
- These results will be saved for you to view later through the Historical Graph.
- You can then choose to go back to the Home Page, Choose Another Player, or Upload More Videos. 

## Consistency
- This is similar to the Compare mode. However, you will not choose any NBA players. Instead you will be asked to upload 5 videos from the side and 5 from the front.
    - ```There are example angles that you should follow as deviating from those can lead to errors in the score.```
- Once the videos are uploaded, it will take a few seconds for the videos to be processed and you will be redirected to a results page.
- The results page will show your overall consistency score across all five shots as well as sub-scores. 
    - These scores will allow you to see how consistent you are across each shot because consistency across your shooting form is important and allows you to see what you need to be more consistent with.
- These results will be saved for you to view later through the Historical Graph.
- You can then choose to go back to the Home Page or Upload More Videos. 

## Historical Graph
- This mode will let you toggle between your front and side sub-scores and your overall score. 
- You can change between Compare and Consistency to see those scores.
- You can also toggle between the past day, the last 2 days, the last week, and the last month of scores to track your improvement. 
- The last day and 2 days have breaks to show you when a new day has been selected and go by the hour.
- The last week and month only show a view dates in the x-axis but are day increments and contain the highest value for a specific day.
- You can also select points to see the specific date and time as well as the actual value.
